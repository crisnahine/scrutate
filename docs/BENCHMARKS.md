## Caveats (read these)

- ugrep was measured from the build bundled with another program on this machine (`argv[0]` dispatch), compiled with `+sse2` only; a native ugrep build with AVX2/AVX-512 would be faster. ack and ag were not available (building them from source is not possible in this environment). Their rows are absent rather than estimated.
- Two logical cores. Multi-threaded tools (scrutate, ripgrep, ugrep, git grep) scale with cores; GNU grep is single-threaded by design.
- The corpora are Python-only; the byte-level search cost does not depend on the language, but structural-search and indexing figures do.
- Where scrutate is slower, it says so in the tables above: on output-heavy queries (hundreds of thousands of matching lines) its single formatting thread trails ripgrep (up to 25 % with 1.5 M matching lines), and ast-grep is faster on structural search. See docs/ROADMAP.md.
